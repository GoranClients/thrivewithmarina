Sekcija 2 — Priča

My journey

A story told
through the body.

Where I came from The turning point What I've witnessed My mission today
01

Where I came from

I didn't come to this work through a certification. I came through my own body. Growing up, I learned that performance was survival — that to be worthy, you had to push, produce, and prove. My body became the thing I managed, not the home I lived in.

For years I chased health through discipline. I worked out harder, ate cleaner, meditated longer. And still something felt off — a low-grade exhaustion that no routine could fix, a sense of being slightly outside of myself no matter what I did.

"I was doing everything 'right' — and still couldn't feel truly well. That gap became the question that changed everything."
02

The turning point

The turning point came when I started studying the nervous system — not just as anatomy, but as the living architecture of how we feel safe, create, love, and lead. I discovered breathwork, somatic movement, and the science of trauma and regulation.

I realized that everything I had been working so hard to fix on the outside was actually asking to be healed from within. The body wasn't the problem. The body was the portal.

"You can't think your way into a thriving body. You have to feel your way back."

Over the next years, I immersed myself in every methodology that could help me understand this connection — from biohacking and neuroscience to trauma-informed somatic work and breathwork facilitation. I became my own most devoted student.

03

What I've witnessed

Over 16 years working with leaders, entrepreneurs, and women in transition, I've seen the same pattern again and again: brilliant, driven women whose bodies are quietly screaming for a different way — but who haven't been given the tools to listen.

Women who have built businesses, led teams, and created real impact in the world — while quietly running on adrenaline, disconnected from their own bodies, leading from fear instead of from truth.

"The most successful women I know are often the most exhausted. And the solution is never more strategy — it's always more safety."
04

My mission today

My work isn't about fixing you. You are not broken. It's about returning you to yourself — so you can lead, create, and love from a place of genuine wholeness, not performance.

I believe the world changes when women lead from a regulated nervous system and a vital, nourished body. That is the foundation I help women build — one breath, one session, one embodied decision at a time.

What I believe

The principles that
guide everything.

01

The body is the doorway

Real transformation doesn't happen through the mind alone. The body holds the truth — and the body is where lasting change begins. Not as a project to be managed, but as a home to be inhabited.
