Sekcija 3 — Zašto breathwork (pristupačno)

The science

Why breathwork? Because your breath is the fastest path to your nervous system.

Most of us have been breathing in a way that quietly keeps us in stress mode — shallow, rushed, held. We don't notice it because it becomes background noise. But the nervous system notices.

Research shows that over 80% of communication between the body and the brain travels upward — from body to mind. This means that changing your breath directly changes your brain state.

When your breath is regulated, your decisions become clearer. Your relationships deepen. Your creativity returns. You stop leading from fear and start leading from genuine coherence — from the inside out.

Sekcija 6 — FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need
to know

I've never done breathwork before. Is this right for me? ↑

Absolutely. Most of my clients come to HeartLead without any breathwork experience. We start exactly where you are and build at your pace. The practice is designed to be safe, accessible, and deeply effective regardless of your background.

How is this different from therapy or regular coaching? ↓

HeartLead works through the body, not just the mind. While therapy and traditional coaching focus on insight and strategy, somatic breathwork creates shifts at the physiological level — rewiring the nervous system patterns that no amount of talking or thinking can reach on their own.

How do we work together — online or in person? ↓

All sessions are conducted online via video call, which means this program is available to you wherever you are in the world. The breathwork practices are equally powerful in this format.

How do I know which program — 6 or 12 weeks — is right for me? ↓

We figure that out together on the Clarity Call. I'll ask you where you are, what you're carrying, and what you're working toward — and we'll choose the container that truly fits.
